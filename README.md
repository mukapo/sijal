# Sijal

> ## Where poetry becomes a duel  
> **Verse against verse, until the Sijal is decided.**

**Sijal** is an Arabic competitive game built around poetry.

It transforms poetic exchange from a passing interaction into a structured competitive experience — one where every encounter contributes to a larger journey of progression, identity, and status.

Sijal is being designed as an Arabic-first world where poetry is not simply content to consume.

**Poetry is the game itself.**

---

## Live Preview

Explore the first public expression of Sijal:

**https://mukapo.github.io/sijal/**

The current experience is an early product and brand preview. It introduces the visual identity, core competitive idea, and the broader world we are building around Arabic poetry.

---

## What is Sijal?

Poetic challenges and verbal duels have long existed across Arabic culture and continue to appear today across communities, social platforms, and digital experiences.

The behavior already exists.

What is often missing is a world that brings it together.

Sijal turns that behavior into a connected competitive journey.

A poetic exchange is no longer an isolated moment. It becomes part of a larger system where players compete, progress, build their identity, and establish their place within a living Arabic world.

---

## The Core Experience

At the heart of Sijal is a simple idea:

**A verse is presented. A verse answers it. The duel continues until the Sijal is decided.**

From that foundation, the experience can grow into something much larger:

- competitive poetic encounters
- player progression
- identity and reputation
- meaningful status
- recurring challenges
- a world players can return to

The goal is not to place conventional game mechanics on top of poetry.

The goal is to make **language, response, creativity, and poetic competition** the mechanics themselves.

---

## Our Vision

We want Sijal to become a place where people do more than read poetry.

A place where they participate.

Where they respond.

Where they compete.

Where they build a presence through language.

The ambition is to create a complete Arabic competitive world that begins with a single verse, but does not end there.

**From a poetic duel to a complete journey.**

---

## Why Sijal?

Arabic poetry already contains many of the qualities that make competitive experiences compelling:

**creativity, expression, response, challenge, identity, mastery, and recognition.**

Sijal brings those qualities into one connected experience.

Rather than treating Arabic poetry as static cultural content, we are exploring how it can become an active, social, and repeatable form of play.

The opportunity is not to reinvent poetry.

It is to build a world around a behavior that already exists.

---

## Arabic First

Sijal is not a product designed elsewhere and later translated into Arabic.

Arabic is the starting point.

The language, typography, visual rhythm, interface direction, cultural references, and overall identity are being developed around an Arabic experience from the beginning.

The visual direction draws from the richness of Arabic manuscripts, geometry, ink, paper, and classical composition — without turning the product into a historical recreation.

The aim is an identity that feels both:

**authentically Arabic and distinctly contemporary.**

---

## More Than a Match

Sijal is designed around the idea that a single poetic encounter should lead to something larger.

Each competition can become part of a player's wider journey.

Over time, that journey can shape:

**who the player is, how they progress, who they face, and the place they build for themselves within Sijal.**

That is the broader product vision:

not simply a tool for exchanging verses, but a world built around poetic competition.

## Current Stage

Sijal is currently in early development.

- the clarity of the product idea
- the Arabic-first identity

- the visual direction

---

## Technology

The current Sijal web experience is built with a modern web stack focused on performance, responsiveness, and maintainability.

At a high level, it uses:

- **Next.js**
- **React**
- **TypeScript**
- **Custom responsive styling**
- **GitHub Actions**
- **GitHub Pages**

The current website is delivered as a lightweight static experience and is designed to work across mobile, tablet, and desktop.

The game itself is a separate product track currently under development.
